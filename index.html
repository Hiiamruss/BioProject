Puberty: The art of Growing Up

Introduction

We all experience it, sudden changes, sometimes even embarrasing ones. This means we are in a phase called Puberty. Puberty is a phase where children from ages 9-14 (may vary between genders) develop and grow to become a mature adult. This includes physical changes and also mental ones.
